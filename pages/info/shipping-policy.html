Shipping Policy

Last Updated: November 4, 2025

1. Shipping Options

We offer several shipping options to meet your needs:

Shipping Method Delivery Time Cost
Standard Shipping 5-7 business days $5.99 (FREE over $100)
Express Shipping 2-3 business days $12.99
Overnight Shipping 1 business day $24.99
International Shipping 10-15 business days Varies by location

Free Shipping: Enjoy free standard shipping on all orders over $100! This offer applies to domestic orders only.

2. Processing Time

Orders are typically processed within 1-2 business days (Monday-Friday, excluding holidays). Orders placed on weekends or holidays will be processed the next business day.

During peak seasons (holidays, sales events), processing times may be slightly longer. We appreciate your patience during these times.

3. Domestic Shipping

United States

We ship to all 50 states, including Alaska and Hawaii, as well as US territories. Please note that shipping to Alaska, Hawaii, and US territories may take longer than standard delivery times.

Shipping Carriers

We use trusted shipping carriers including:

  • USPS (United States Postal Service)
  • UPS (United Parcel Service)
  • FedEx

The carrier used will depend on your location and the shipping method selected.

4. International Shipping

We currently ship to over 50 countries worldwide. International shipping rates and delivery times vary depending on the destination country.

Customs and Duties

International orders may be subject to import duties, taxes, and customs fees. These charges are the responsibility of the recipient and are not included in the product or shipping costs. We recommend checking with your local customs office for more information about potential fees before placing an order.

International Delivery Time

International delivery typically takes 10-15 business days, but may take longer depending on customs processing and local postal services.

5. Order Tracking

Once your order ships, you will receive a shipping confirmation email with tracking information. You can track your package by:

  • Clicking the tracking link in your shipping confirmation email
  • Logging into your account and viewing your Order History
  • Visiting the carrier's website with your tracking number

Please allow 24 hours for tracking information to update after receiving your shipping confirmation.

6. Shipping Restrictions

We are unable to ship to:

  • PO Boxes for Express or Overnight shipping
  • APO/FPO addresses (limited to Standard shipping only)
  • Countries with shipping embargoes

7. Missing or Delayed Orders

Delayed Orders

If your order hasn't arrived within the estimated delivery timeframe, please:

  • Check your tracking information for the latest updates
  • Verify that the shipping address was entered correctly
  • Contact the shipping carrier directly for more information
  • Contact our customer service team if the issue persists

Lost Packages

If your tracking information shows that your package was delivered but you haven't received it:

  • Check with neighbors or building management
  • Look around your delivery address (sometimes packages are left in safe locations)
  • Wait 48 hours (sometimes packages are marked delivered early)
  • Contact the carrier to file a claim
  • Contact our customer service team for assistance

8. Address Changes

If you need to change your shipping address, please contact us immediately. Address changes can only be made within 2 hours of order placement, before the order has been processed.

Once an order has shipped, we cannot modify the shipping address. You may be able to contact the carrier directly to request a delivery redirect.

9. Signature Required

For orders over $200, we may require a signature upon delivery for security purposes. Please ensure someone is available to sign for the package.

10. Holiday Shipping

During peak holiday seasons, we recommend ordering early to ensure delivery by your desired date. Holiday shipping cutoff dates will be posted on our website and social media channels.

Please note that carrier delays may occur during peak seasons due to increased shipping volume.
