Return Policy

Last Updated: November 4, 2024

Our Promise: We want you to love your FashionFlex purchase! If you're not completely satisfied, we offer a hassle-free 30-day return policy.

How to Return

1

Log In

Sign into your account and go to Order History

2

Request Return

Select the items you'd like to return and reason

3

Print Label

Print your prepaid return shipping label

4

Ship It

Pack items securely and drop off at carrier location

1. Return Timeframe

You have 30 days from the date of delivery to initiate a return. Returns requested after 30 days cannot be accepted.

The 30-day window begins on the date you receive your order, not the date it was shipped.

2. Return Eligibility

To be eligible for a return, items must meet the following conditions:

  • Unworn and unwashed: Items must be in their original condition
  • Tags attached: All original tags must be intact and attached
  • Original packaging: If applicable, items should include original packaging
  • No damage: Items must be free from stains, odors, pet hair, or damage

Non-Returnable Items

For hygiene and safety reasons, the following items cannot be returned:

  • Underwear and intimates
  • Swimwear (if hygiene liner has been removed)
  • Earrings
  • Beauty products and cosmetics
  • Final sale items (marked as such at time of purchase)
  • Gift cards

3. Return Shipping

Domestic Returns (US)

Returns within the United States are FREE! We provide a prepaid return shipping label with your return authorization. Simply print the label, attach it to your package, and drop it off at any carrier location.

International Returns

For international returns, customers are responsible for return shipping costs. We recommend using a trackable shipping method and purchasing shipping insurance for valuable items.

4. Refunds

Processing Time

Once we receive your return, please allow:

  • 2-3 business days for inspection
  • 5-7 business days for refund processing

You will receive an email confirmation when your refund has been processed.

Refund Method

Refunds will be issued to the original form of payment:

  • Credit/Debit Cards: Refund will appear on your statement within 5-7 business days
  • PayPal: Refund will be credited to your PayPal account within 2-3 business days
  • Gift Cards: Refund will be issued as store credit

Shipping Costs

Original shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error (e.g., wrong item shipped, defective product).

5. Exchanges

We offer FREE exchanges for different sizes or colors of the same item!

How to Exchange

  1. Initiate a return and select "Exchange" as your reason
  2. Choose the size or color you'd like to receive
  3. Ship your original item back using the prepaid label
  4. We'll ship your exchange item as soon as we receive your return

If the exchange item is a different price, we'll refund or charge the difference.

6. Damaged or Defective Items

We inspect all items before shipping, but if you receive a damaged or defective item, please contact us immediately.

What to Do

  • Contact customer service within 48 hours of delivery
  • Provide photos of the damage or defect
  • Include your order number

We'll arrange for a replacement or full refund, including original shipping costs. You won't need to return the damaged item unless we specifically request it.

7. Wrong Item Received

If you received the wrong item, please contact us immediately. We'll send you the correct item at no additional cost and provide a prepaid label to return the incorrect item.

8. Sale and Promotional Items

Sale Items

Items purchased on sale are eligible for return or exchange within the standard 30-day window, unless marked as "Final Sale."

Final Sale Items

Items marked as "Final Sale" cannot be returned or exchanged. These items are clearly marked on the product page and at checkout.

Promotional Items

If you received a promotional gift with your order and you return your purchase, the promotional item must also be returned.

9. Store Credit

Instead of a refund, you may opt to receive store credit. Store credit benefits include:

  • Receive 10% additional credit (e.g., $100 refund = $110 store credit)
  • Store credit never expires
  • Can be used on any future purchase

10. Partial Returns

If you received free shipping due to meeting a minimum order threshold and you return part of your order, resulting in the remaining items falling below that threshold, the original shipping fee will be deducted from your refund.

11. Return Tips

  • Keep tracking number: Save your return tracking number until your refund is processed
  • Pack securely: Use sturdy packaging to prevent damage during transit
  • Include paperwork: Include your return authorization form (emailed to you)
  • Remove old labels: Cover or remove any old shipping labels from the box

Need Help with a Return?

Our customer service team is here to help make your return as easy as possible.
