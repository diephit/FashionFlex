Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our products, shipping, returns, and more.

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping typically takes 5-7 business days. Express shipping is available for 2-3 business days delivery. Free shipping is offered on all orders over $100.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, we ship to over 50 countries worldwide. International shipping times vary by location but typically take 10-15 business days. Additional customs fees may apply depending on your country.

How can I track my order?

Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking number via email. You can also track your order by logging into your account and visiting the Order History section.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day return policy for unworn items with original tags attached. Items must be in their original condition. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive your return.

How do I initiate a return?

Log into your account, go to Order History, and select the order you want to return. Click "Request Return" and follow the instructions. You'll receive a prepaid return label via email.

Can I exchange an item?

Yes! We offer free exchanges for different sizes or colors. Simply initiate a return and select "Exchange" as your reason. We'll ship your new item as soon as we receive your return.

Can I cancel my order?

Orders can be cancelled within 2 hours of placement. After that, orders are processed for shipping and cannot be cancelled. Please contact customer service immediately if you need to cancel.

Can I change my shipping address?

Shipping addresses can only be changed within 2 hours of order placement. Contact our customer service team immediately with your order number and new address.

Do you offer gift wrapping?

Yes, we offer gift wrapping for $5 per item. You can select this option during checkout. We'll also include a personalized gift message if you'd like.

How do I choose the right size?

Please refer to our Size Guide available on each product page. We provide detailed measurements for all our items. If you're between sizes, we recommend sizing up for a comfortable fit.

How do I care for my garments?

Care instructions are provided on the label of each garment. Generally, we recommend washing in cold water and hanging to dry to maintain quality. Avoid bleach and high heat.

Are your products sustainable?

We're committed to sustainability! Our collection includes items made from organic cotton, recycled materials, and eco-friendly fabrics. Look for the "Sustainable" tag on product pages.

Still have questions?

Can't find the answer you're looking for? Our customer service team is here to help.
